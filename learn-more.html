About Neo AI Framework

Our Mission

Neo AI is an advanced, hybrid explainable deep learning framework specifically designed for dermatological diagnostics. Our mission is to bridge the gap between cutting-edge artificial intelligence and clinical dermatology, providing doctors and patients with rapid, reliable, and transparent skin disease assessments.

How It Works

Our platform utilizes a hybrid pipeline combining robust feature extraction with state-of-the-art vision models. When an image is uploaded:

  • OOD Detection: A built-in heuristic filter checks if the image is actually of human skin, instantly rejecting irrelevant objects like bottles or keyboards.
  • Classification: The image is securely processed to determine the top possible dermatological conditions from our database of over 20 diseases.
  • Explainable AI (XAI): We use a modified Grad-CAM (Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping) algorithm to highlight exactly which pixels influenced the model's decision, ensuring total transparency.

AI Assistant Integration

To assist users with understanding their results, Neo AI features a clinical chatbot powered by high-speed inference Llama models. The assistant provides context-aware, medical-disclaimer-backed explanations for user queries regarding their symptoms and XAI heatmap outputs.
